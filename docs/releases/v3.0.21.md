# SWI3S Studio v3.0.21

A Link that goes inactive and is brought up again now decodes again from its new start
sequence, and a capture whose clock only paused decodes on through the pause. Any pane can
be maximized to fill the window and put back with one key, the Registers pane filters by
where each value came from, and zoomed in far enough the Audio pane draws the samples
themselves. The app quits when its window closes, and Ctrl+C in the terminal quits it too.
PySide6 6.12 is supported again.

## Decode

- **A Link brought up again decodes again.** After a Link's clock stops for more than 1 ms,
  each later Cold or Warm Start is found, in a capture that starts in audio mode as well (a
  large capture opened as a time window), and the decode starts again where its audio
  begins. That gives a new region labelled by its PHY on the Timeline, registers reset by a
  Cold Start and kept by a Warm Start, and **Link Inactive** in the Bus Grid while the clock
  is stopped. Nothing is decoded while the Link is inactive, and the Registers pane's PHY
  block follows the bring-up at the cursor. PHY3 (DLV) captures restart too: the
  recovered clock locks again at the new bring-up's first Row Sync. A bring-up that switches
  a Link between a forwarded-clock PHY and DLV is not followed.
- **A detection the wire contradicts is dropped**: audio after a stop, a line that bursts
  and pauses, or a "bring-up" after which no CRC-valid command decodes while one does
  without it.
- **A clock that only paused decodes on through the pause.** Audio mode never stops its
  clock, so a stop with audio after it and no bring-up means samples were lost or two
  captures were joined. If the bus carried on in phase, the decode goes on as if nothing
  happened. If it did not, the decode re-detects the column count and phase there, as for a
  capture that begins mid-stream.
- **A config CSV keeps its ports through a later bring-up**, and a Warm Start that keeps
  the geometry puts its ports on the new phase.
- The native decode core changed (`score_abi` 15), so it must be rebuilt
  (`bash native/build_local.sh`, or `run.sh`/`run.ps1`, which rebuild a stale core).
- `api.decode_capture` decodes exactly as the app does, later bring-ups included.

## Window

- **Maximize a pane.** Click in a pane and press ⌘⇧M (Ctrl+Shift+M on Windows and Linux),
  use View ▸ Maximize Pane, the ⤢ button at the right of its title bar, or a double-click
  on the title bar. The pane fills the window, and the same again puts every pane back as it
  was: sizes, tab groups and floating panes, with that pane in front and keeping the focus.
  - Commands, Registers, Bus Grid, CDS and Samples keep the Timeline above them to navigate
    with; Audio, Capture, Timing, Statistics and Bookmarks take the whole window.
  - The Timeline itself is never maximized.
  - Showing, hiding or closing a pane, adding or removing a Link, leaving the Bus Analyzer
    or opening a workspace puts the panes back first.
  - A workspace saved while a pane is maximized keeps the arrangement to return to.
  - A double-click on a title bar no longer floats a pane; drag it out to float it.
- **The app quits when its window closes**, even while a window with no parent (such as
  pyqtgraph's Export dialog from a plot's right-click menu) is still open.
- **Ctrl+C in the launching terminal quits the app** as File ▸ Quit does, even while it is
  idle, and a second Ctrl+C ends a quit that hangs.
- **The Registers pane filters by provenance.** Its colour key is the filter: click Cold
  Reset, Bus Write, Bus Read, CSV Import or Manual Edit to hide or show the registers whose
  value came from it, so Cold Reset off leaves only what something set. The key entries
  work from the keyboard. The filter survives cursor moves and is saved in the workspace,
  and so are the blocks you leave open or closed while filtering. A filter that leaves
  nothing says so.
- **With no capture open, every menu item that needs one is greyed out**, submenus and
  their keys included; opening files, preferences, the playback device and the View and
  Help menus stay available. Commands ▸ Filter and Decode ▸ Manual SSP Move stay greyed when
  the Bus Analyzer is entered.
- A tree section's open / closed arrow is drawn in its heading's colour, so the Registers
  sections show their arrows on the dark theme.
- Copying register rows from two blocks gives a line each, in tree order, instead of
  merging rows that shared a number.
- **A reopened workspace names its vendor registers** in the Commands table, from each
  Link's device register maps.

## Audio and Capture

- **Zoomed in, Audio is drawn as its samples.** Once each sample has about 6 pixels of the
  track's width, a track draws a stem from zero to each sample's value with a dot on it,
  placed where the sample arrived on the bus; zoomed out again it is the line through them.
- **Redrawing the Audio tracks keeps the zoom.** Turning a spectrogram on or off, changing
  a frame size, showing or hiding a channel, colours, names, or Filter & Gain no longer
  move the view to the cursor. A long track title in a narrow pane no longer widens its
  track or puts it on a different stretch of the capture.
- The Audio line is 2 px by default, as the Capture lines are; a weight set in View ▸
  Waveform Colors and Line Weight… is kept.
- **A stopped line draws its true level at every zoom** in the Capture pane. The zoomed-out
  view is also faster on very large captures.

## Checks and packaging

- **PySide6 6.12 is supported again.** The app and the test session delete the widgets
  PySide 6.12 leaves owned by Python before PySide's own exit sweep, so the process no longer
  crashes as the interpreter exits, and the version cap is lifted.
- The perf run ends with a `perf margins` section: each timed check's median and worst as a
  fraction of its ceiling, pass or fail.
- `pip install .` builds: `pyproject.toml`'s `data-files` is in a form setuptools accepts,
  and the theme's icons ship in the wheel. A test validates the metadata.
