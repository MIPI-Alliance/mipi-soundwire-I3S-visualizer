# SWI3S Studio v3.0.6

Large-capture performance, plus PDM rate and SSPA reconfigure decode fixes.

## Large captures

- The Decoded Samples pane loads a window of rows on demand and evicts rows outside it.
- Filtered-sample queries are memoised.
- The timeline paints a decimated view.
- The TX raster and the persistence view are cached.
- The Symbols pane builds only the rows inside its window.

## Fixes

- The PDM derived-rate estimator reports the correct rate.
- An SSPA with an immediate reconfigure decodes correctly (native core).
- `run.ps1` no longer aborts on the probe that checks for a stale native core.
